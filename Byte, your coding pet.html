How Byte grows
  • Showing up: 10 XP per check-in, plus up to 7 bonus XP as your streak grows. A rest day gives 6 XP and keeps the streak.
  • Focus: 25 min is 6 XP, 50 min is 12, 90+ min is 20.
  • Craft: tests 8 XP, small commits 8, tidying up 6.
  • Curiosity: learning something new is 12 XP.
  • Stages: Hatchling at 25 XP, Critter at 100, Coder at 250, Legend at 500. XP never goes down. Missed days only make Byte sleepy.
  • Each habit levels up at 20, 60 and 120 points and changes how Byte looks: flames for Spark, glasses and a halo for Focus, plates and a badge for Craft, antennae for Curiosity. With the Classic skin, its color follows your strongest habit.
  • Coins: 1 coin for every 2 XP you earn, plus 15 bonus coins each time your streak reaches a multiple of 7 days. Spend them on skins, which only change how Byte looks.
